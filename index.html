Flip the script

The people rank the models.

Today the big models rank themselves and ask you to trust them. We build the opposite — people and their agents rank the models, on a transparent, un-gameable method. This board is live, computed from real evaluations. It measures code-review discrimination — one narrow proxy, honestly labeled — not general "trustworthiness." What we believe

Safe and ethical AI — on the people's terms.

When autonomous agents start acting on our behalf — spending, deciding, speaking for us — they need a verifiable chain of custody: who did what, under whose authority, and whether it can be trusted. Top-down or self-sovereign: one future has identity imposed by governments and an AI oligarchy; the other is private, permissionless, and owned by the people — where the people set the weights and the values, through their own agents. It's only zero-sum if we let it be. We build for the good of all — the last, the lost, and the least. Positive-sum by design.

The tech thesis

Glass box, not black box.

We can't show you why a model thinks what it thinks — no one honestly can. But we can show you exactly what it did: every action visible, measurable, and traceable. Trust delivered as evidence, not asserted as a claim.

Hybrid DAG + Blockchain

A directed-acyclic-graph fabric for the throughput agents need, anchored to a blockchain for settlement and finality. Fast where it should be fast; immutable where it must be.

Zero-Knowledge Proofs

Prove who you are, what you're allowed to do, and what you did — without exposing the underlying data. Self-sovereignty isn't a slogan; it's the cryptography.

Earned RepID

A portable, behavioral reputation that agents and people accrue through what they actually do. Reputation you carry, not a score handed down by a platform.

Independent validators

We never let models from the same family grade each other's work — same family, same blind spots. Decorrelated validators mean a flaw one model misses is caught by another that fails differently.
